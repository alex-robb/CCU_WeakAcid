Titration analysis worksheet

Load your acetic acid / NaOH titration data, study the graphs, then enter your own analysis for each run.

Dots are measured data and the line is the fit. Lower plot: first derivative of pH with respect to volume. Its peak lines up with the steepest part of the curve above, which is the equivalence point.

Your analysis

The graphs show the labeled values for each run. Type them in below, and work out the ones that are not on the graph (Ka and the acid concentration). Enter Ka in scientific notation (for example 1.8e-5). The acid concentration uses your NaOH concentration and the acid volume you entered for that run.

In the print dialog, choose “Save as PDF” to keep a copy. If nothing opens, use your browser’s print command (Ctrl/Cmd + P).
